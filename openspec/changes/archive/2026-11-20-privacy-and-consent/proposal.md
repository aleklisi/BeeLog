# Proposal: Privacy and consent information

## Why

BeeLog needs clear, accessible information about cookies and similar technologies, personal-data processing, and the choices available to users. The project should define these areas before implementing the corresponding notices and controls.

## What Changes

- Define user-facing cookie and similar-technology information.
- Define a privacy policy covering personal-data processing and data-subject rights (RODO/GDPR).
- Define how users are informed about, provide, and change consent or other applicable preferences.
- Identify any additional privacy or consent notices required by the product.

This change records the scope only. The detailed legal content, applicable technologies, consent categories, retention, and implementation behavior remain to be specified after reviewing the product's data flows and legal requirements.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `privacy-and-consent`: User-facing privacy information and consent or preference controls.

## Impact

- Future privacy notices, policy pages, and consent controls in the frontend.
- Future review of cookies, storage, analytics, authentication, and other personal-data flows.
- OpenSpec capability requirements to be refined before implementation.
