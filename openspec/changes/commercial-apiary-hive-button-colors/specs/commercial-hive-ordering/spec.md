# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Commercial hive cards provide visible drag feedback
Commercial hive cards and their drag affordance MUST remain distinguishable from surrounding content in light and dark themes, with a visible indication while a card is being dragged.

#### Scenario: Drag a hive in either theme
- **WHEN** a user drags a commercial hive card in light or dark theme
- **THEN** the drag affordance remains legible and the dragged card receives a clear visual highlight
